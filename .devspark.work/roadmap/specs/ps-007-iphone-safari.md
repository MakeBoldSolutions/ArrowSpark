# ps-007: iPhone Safari

## Outcome

The game has been played on a real iPhone in both orientations, what breaks there is fixed, and the
site states the result.

## Current Reality

The owner reported on 2026-11-08 a rough test on a real iPhone, in portrait: the final feedback was
that the game was playable. It was not a structured review and should not be read as one. The site
and durable docs still say no iPhone has been tried (for example Part 12 of the story,
`web/src/content/evidence.yaml` and `.knowledge/reference/touch-gestures.md`).

## Scope and Independence

In scope: a proper iPhone test session, covering portrait and landscape and recording the browser and
iOS version, fixing defects that block play, and updating the site's and docs' claims to match what
was observed.

Out of scope: an App Store build, and devices beyond the one tested.

Standalone boundary: with later items canceled, iPhone support is honestly stated, whether that means
verified, or known issues and a clear message.

## Future Health

Web storage on iOS can be cleared by the browser, which matters for ps-001's saved progress, so record
what is observed. Emulation does not count as evidence (constitution Principle V).

## Dependencies and Decisions

No delivered prerequisites. The owner will schedule more iPhone testing later, so this is not near-term
work.

## Completion Expectations

The owner has played on a real iPhone in both orientations with results recorded, including browser
and iOS version. Defects that block play are fixed or the limit is stated on the site. Site and
knowledge claims match what was observed.
