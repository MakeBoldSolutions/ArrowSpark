# ps-008: First outside playtest

## Outcome

People other than the designer have played the fuller game, and a written report says what they did
and thought, including whether they found their way without help.

## Current Reality

The owner has done nearly all the playing, and the rough iPhone test on 2026-11-08 does not count as a
playtest. The first-time discovery check was deferred from the Reference Knot work and has never been
run. The reaction endpoint accepts requests from the site, but
no reaction has been verified end to end. Its original 14-day learning window has passed.

## Scope and Independence

In scope: confirming feedback arrives, a new time-boxed window, a first group of new players with at
least some sessions observed, and a report that quotes negative reactions as given.

Out of scope: changing the game as part of this item; follow-up work becomes new items.

Standalone boundary: with later items canceled, the project has its first independent evidence.

## Future Health

Findings should feed level tuning and may change the order of remaining work. If ps-004 has shipped,
compare what players said with what they did.

## Dependencies and Decisions

Depends on ps-001 and ps-005, because the owner judges the game not ready for outside players yet. The
owner decides when it is ready and who plays; the dependencies can be revised then.

## Completion Expectations

An agreed number of new players have played. The report answers the discovery question and states how
small the sample is. Reaction data is handled as the reactions requirements promise.
