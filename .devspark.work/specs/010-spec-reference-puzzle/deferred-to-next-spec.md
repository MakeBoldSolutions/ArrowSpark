# Deferred to the next spec: fresh-player validation protocol (working note)

Origin: SC-009 of the Reference Puzzle spec was NOT performed and was deferred by the owner on 2026-10-01.
Reason: The project currently has one active designer/tester. Rather than recruit someone solely to satisfy a development gate, fresh-player validation will be performed when the game is published as a Web Showcase, where multiple genuinely new players can encounter it naturally.

What the Reference Puzzle spec can and cannot claim:
- The Reference Puzzle was tested by its designer, who was familiar with earlier versions.
- No independent player has yet validated the experience.
- Outside-player validation is future work.

Requirements the next spec should inherit:
- Fresh players receive minimal guidance. They are not taught the project's internal design vocabulary (for example neighborhoods, bridge arrows, discovery beats) and are not told what to look for before playing.
- A player's first exposure is preserved: do not coach, do not explain Level Select or groups, do not hint at what the Reference Puzzle contains.
- Observe, then interview conversationally, one question at a time. The interviewer owns the checklist and maps answers to it; the player is not asked to fill in a form or format answers.
- Derive objective information from the game where possible; ask the player only for what needs human observation.
- Record the player's words as given, including negative ones; do not reinterpret ambiguous answers favorably.

What to observe about discovery (the original SC-009 intent):
- Whether the player opens Level Select unprompted, and whether they notice and choose the ArrowSpark Levels group.
- Roughly how long that took, which entry they started with, what they said, and anything the facilitator did that could have guided them.

Other questions the owner expects a showcase to answer: first-time discovery, comprehension of Level Select, discoverability of the ArrowSpark Levels group, first-player reactions, unguided play, whether the intended experience survives first contact, challenge and satisfaction, and willingness to play another level.

Also carried forward: the numeric on-screen cell-size evidence and the F3 readout issue belong to this spec's desktop verification, not the next spec.
